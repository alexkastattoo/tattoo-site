03 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before the ink.

How much does a tattoo cost?+

Pricing depends on the size, placement, level of detail and time needed. Send your idea and references for an individual estimate before booking. My minimum is $200/hour or $1000 per session.

How do I book an appointment?+

To book a tattoo session, please fill out the booking form on this website or send me a message on Instagram @alexkastattoo. You also can email to alexkastattoo@gmail.com. I usually respond within 1–3 days.

Do I need to leave a deposit?+

Yes. A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking. The deposit goes toward the final cost of your tattoo.

Can I reschedule my appointment?+

Yes, but please notify me at least 48 hours in advance. Short-notice reschedules may result in losing your deposit.

How long does a session take?+

Session length depends on the project and placement. Larger or more detailed pieces may need multiple appointments. We will discuss the estimated time before booking.

Does it hurt?+

Yes, tattoos involve discomfort — but it’s manageable. Everyone feels it differently, and I always make sure you’re as comfortable as possible throughout the session.
